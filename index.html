What we do

Two forms of dialogue, one conversation.

Events raise questions for the journal. Published essays shape the next event.

01 · Written dialogue

The Science in Society Review

An outlet for undergraduate analytical publication. Every connection and comparison starts with a professional launchpad towards articulating academic ideas, based on question-driven, analysis-based journalism and publications that pair frontier development within the context of institutional actors.

  • —Citation-based analysis, work towards measurable societal impact, and research translations
  • —Two themed issues each year, plus rolling online releases
  • —Additional publicity towards local newsletters and think tanks

02 · Voice dialogue

Fireside chats & debates

Inspired by the Hopkins Bloomberg Center debates in D.C., brought to Homewood: faculty from different departments alongside leaders from industry, philanthropy and government, on one shared question.

  • —Multi-speaker events that map the ecosystem of collaboration
  • —Where disciplines agree, and where they pull against each other
  • —Additional publicity towards podcasts and media
